Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

PangPang Cat: The Last Honeychu (the “Game”) does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. For rankings and in-game mail, the Game also uses a simple game account on our server (see “Game server” below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for example, to double rewards or revive) and occasional full-screen ads between battles. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Shop → “Ad Privacy Settings”. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Game server (rankings and mail)

When you start the Game, it creates a random ID on your device (not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID) and signs in to our server (sinnaru83.cafe24.com) with it. The server stores: this random ID, a ranking nickname (randomly assigned, you can change it), your combat power, best stage, Endless Cat Tower floor and play records used to check rankings, app version and language, and the times you last connected. Your nickname, rank and records are shown to other players in the rankings. The server also keeps mail (gifts) sent to you and whether you claimed it, and may restrict accounts that cheat. For security and to prevent abuse, the server may briefly process your IP address; it is not stored with your game account.

Data is kept until you ask us to delete it or the service ends. To delete your game server data at any time, e-mail us with your ranking nickname (shown in the Ranking screen); we delete it within 30 days. Deleting the Game alone does not delete server data.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Monthly Pass) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. The Game only receives a purchase record from Google Play to deliver and restore your items.

Network use

The Game connects to the internet for ads, Google Play purchases and app update checks, and to our game server for rankings and mail as described above.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
