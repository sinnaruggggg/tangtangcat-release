Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

PangPang Cat: The Last Honeychu (the “Game”) does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location, and has no account system. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for example, to double rewards or revive) and occasional full-screen ads between battles. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Shop → “Ad Privacy Settings”. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Monthly Pass) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. The Game only receives a purchase record from Google Play to deliver and restore your items.

Network use

The Game connects to the internet only for ads, Google Play purchases and, in versions installed outside Google Play, checking for app updates on a GitHub Pages server (no information identifying you is sent for update checks).

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
