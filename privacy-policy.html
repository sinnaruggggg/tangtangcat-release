Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

TangTangCat: The Last Churu (the “Game”) does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information of its users.

Information we collect

The Game has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or in-app purchases, and does not access location, contacts, camera or microphone. Game progress and settings are stored only in local storage on the user's device.

Network use

The Google Play version of the Game does not connect to any server. Versions installed outside Google Play may connect over HTTPS to a GitHub Pages server only to check for and download app updates; no information identifying the user is sent.

Children's privacy

The Game may be used by people of all ages, including children. Because the Game collects no personal information, it does not collect or share children's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated.
